About Us

TEERANDAZ LABS LLP is a precision technology and engineering startup focused on intelligent automation, embedded systems, IoT solutions, and practical infrastructure technologies. The company develops and deploys systems aimed at solving real-world operational challenges through reliable hardware-software integration.


Its flagship initiative, the Tankiwala System, is designed for smart water filling management and automated pump control, helping optimize water usage, monitoring, and operational reliability across residential and commercial environments.

The company also provides B2B technical consultancy and development support in embedded systems, automation, sensing technologies, electrical systems, software integration, and custom engineering solutions.
